1. **A team management view** (cards = agents)
2. **A chat box** (Talk to All Team)
3. **A meetings system** (another page)
4. **A workspace sidebar (Actions, Meetings, Knowledge)**

…and none of them are connected.

### **1. You send a message in the "chat" but the agents’ answers appear somewhere else (Work → Actions or invisible).**

### **2. You have TWO places where communication happens:**

* The “Talk to All Team” chat
* The “Meetings” interface
  But they are not unified.

### **3. The AI Team page feels like a shelf of agents, not a real HQ full of activity.**

# === HIDDEN ERRORS THAT MUST BE FIXED ===

### ❌ Problem 1: The chat box sends the message but the display feed does not show the agent answers.

**Fix:**
The “Talk to All Team” composer must write into **the same feed** where agents post their own messages.

### ❌ Problem 2: Meetings appear on a different page and feel disconnected.

**Fix:**
Meetings must be displayed **directly inside AI Team** as a panel on top, just like Slack Huddles or Discord voice channels.

### ❌ Problem 3: The feed shows nothing active → user thinks “nothing is working.”

**Fix:**
Seed the feed with:

* Welcome message by Chief Orchestrator
* Intro messages from each agent

**Fix:**
These stay under **Work**, not inside AI Team.
AI Team = conversations and meetings.
