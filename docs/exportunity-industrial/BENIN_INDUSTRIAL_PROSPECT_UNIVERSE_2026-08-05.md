# Benin Industrial Prospect Universe

Checked: 2026-08-05

## Purpose

This is the first source-backed prospect universe for Exportunity Machinery and the Exportunity Industrial OS. It supports private research, qualification and commercial planning for factories, industrial buyers, inventory partners, technical suppliers and logistics operators in Benin.

It is not an exhaustive legal register. It does not establish that a company is currently operating, owns inventory, wants to be contacted, or has approved an Exportunity profile. Every record remains private until staff verifies it.

The executable dataset is in `server/lib/industrial/beninIndustrialProspects.ts` and is available to staff at `/admin/industrial-network`.

## Qualification Rules

1. Open the cited source and confirm the entity still appears there.
2. Verify legal name, operating site, activity, public contact channel and decision-maker role.
3. Classify the record as buyer, manufacturer, inventory partner, technical supplier, logistics partner or institutional partner.
4. Record the specific industrial problem Exportunity could solve; do not use a generic sales pitch.
5. Mark the lead qualified only after relevance is documented.
6. Mark contact-ready only after contact details, lawful basis, message, sender identity and approval are documented.
7. Never claim inventory, capacity, pricing, export readiness or partnership without current evidence.
8. Never create a public factory profile without independent verification and publication approval.

## Coverage Expansion

To move toward a more complete national prospect universe, request current structured lists from APIEx, GDIZ/SIPI, CIPB, CCI Benin and relevant ministries. Then reconcile legal identifiers, phone numbers, websites, locations and operating status before adding records. Google Places can support discovery and location enrichment, but it cannot replace official verification.
