# AGOOJIYE Image Prompts

All generated images must follow this visual identity:

Premium Afrofuturistic Beninese electric mobility brand. Dark graphite and metallic gold palette. Industrial but elegant. Inspired by the Agojie/Amazons of Dahomey without becoming costume-like or violent. No weapons as the central identity. Focus on courage, discipline, movement, engineering, leadership, and African industrial future. Clean cinematic lighting. High contrast. Realistic but slightly futuristic. Suitable for a serious mobility company.
