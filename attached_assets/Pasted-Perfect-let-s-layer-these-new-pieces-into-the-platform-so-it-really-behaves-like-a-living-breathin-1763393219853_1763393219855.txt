## 1. Dynamic Chatrooms & Conversation Naming

### 1.1 Concept

Every internal conversation between agents lives in **chatrooms** (channels + threads), but we don’t want meaningless names like `chat-12345`.

We want:

* **Smart, evolving titles** like:

  * “Initial Outreach Strategy – West Africa”
  * “Investor Update Draft – March 2026”
  * “New Subsidiary Setup – Ghana”

And as the conversation evolves, the **name can change automatically** so humans always understand what’s going on.

### 1.2 Where in the UI

Inside each **Company Workspace**:

* Left sidebar:

  * `Internal Conversations` (already there)

    * `All Channels`
    * `#strategy`
    * `#sales_pipeline`
    * `#finance`
    * `#legal`
    * etc.

When you click a channel, you see:

* **Threads** (each is a conversation, with an evolving name)
* Agents talking to each other
* Summaries + decisions

### 1.3 Dynamic Naming Logic

Every thread has:

* `thread_id`
* `current_title`
* `previous_titles` (history)
* `topic_tags`
* `owner_agent_id` (who started it)
* `created_at`, `updated_at`

**Naming rules:**

1. **Initial Name:**

   * When an agent starts a new conversation, we auto-generate a name from:

     * The **first message**,
     * The **agent’s role**, and
     * The **context** (channel + company type).
   * E.g., if Sales Agent starts:

     > “We need to define our email script for French-speaking logistics leads in Abidjan.”
   * Title becomes:

     * “Email Script – Logistics Leads (Abidjan, FR)”

2. **Automatic Renaming During Evolution:**

   * Every N messages or when the topic shifts significantly, a **Conversation Title Agent** (small internal function) runs:

     * It summarizes the *current* topic,
     * Compares with existing title,
     * If different enough → propose a new title.
   * Example:

     * Old title: “Email Script – Logistics Leads (Abidjan, FR)”
     * After 30 messages, conversation shifts to pricing tiers:

       * New title: “Pricing & Discounts – Logistics Leads (Abidjan, FR)”
   * The old title is stored in `previous_titles` for audit.

3. **Visibility to Humans:**

* Humans (Chairman, employees, investors with access) see:

  * Current title at top.
  * Hover → show “Previously titled: …”
  * Tag chips like `#sales`, `#email_script`, `#pricing`.

4. **Agent-Driven Renames:**

* CEO Orchestrator or any agent can explicitly rename a thread:

  * “I’m renaming this thread to: Q2 Pricing Experiments – Côte d’Ivoire.”
* This is also logged.

## 2. Meetings – Including AI-only Meetings

You want to:

* See **which meetings have been had**,
* Across rooms, agents, and humans,
* And **understand what was discussed**.

### 2.1 Types of Meetings

1. **AI + Human meetings**

   * On Google/Outlook calendar, with real time slots.

2. **AI-only meetings**

   * Scheduled internal “syncs” between agents:

     * They happen as **structured conversations** in a special channel (e.g. `#executive_meetings`).

### 2.2 Where in the UI

In each Company Workspace:

* `Meetings & Calendar` page (we already designed it) now has:

Tabs:

1. **Calendar View**

   * Real calendar (synced with human’s calendar).
   * Shows:

     * Human meetings,
     * Important AI-managed meetings that affect human schedule.

2. **Meeting List**

   * Filters:

     * `Human-only (classic)`,
     * `AI + Human`,
     * `AI-only`.
   * Each row:

     * Title,
     * Participants (agents + humans),
     * Date & time,
     * Channel / thread link,
     * Status (scheduled, completed, cancelled).

3. **Meeting Details**

   * When you click a meeting:

     * Agenda,
     * Participants,
     * Meeting notes (captured by Knowledge Agent),
     * Decisions & tasks,
     * Links to:

       * Internal conversation threads,
       * Related KPIs,
       * Commitments made (e.g. “Increase outreach budget”).

### 2.3 AI-only Meetings

Example:

* “Weekly Sales Review”
  Participants: CEO Orchestrator, Sales Agent, Outreach Agent.

Behaviour:

* Scheduled by CEO Orchestrator.
* At the scheduled time:

  * The system triggers a dedicated internal thread in `#executive_meetings`:

    * Title: “Weekly Sales Review – 2025-11-17.”
  * Agents exchange:

    * Performance numbers,
    * Blockers,
    * Proposals.
  * After conversation:

    * Knowledge Agent writes:

      * Meeting summary,
      * Decisions,
      * Action items (tasks created).

Humans can come later and **read** it like minutes of a meeting.

## 3. Agent Performance Dashboard

You want a **real performance dashboard** like a manager’s HR / KPI cockpit.

### 3.1 Where in the UI

Inside each Company Workspace:

* Left sidebar: add a new item (or a subpage under Agents):

  * `Agent Performance`

And at the **Chairman Console (My Group)** level:

* A **Group Performance** section aggregating all companies & agents.

### 3.2 Company-Level Agent Performance Page

Layout:

* Top filters:

  * Time range (last 7 days / 30 days / quarter).
  * Department (Sales, Finance, Legal, etc.).
  * Agent selection.

Sections:

1. **Performance Overview**

   * Cards:

     * Total meetings booked (by all agents),
     * Total deals closed,
     * Total tasks completed,
     * Total messages sent,
     * Total revenue influenced.

2. **Agent Ranking Table**

   * Columns:

     * Agent name & role,
     * Department,
     * Key KPI (e.g. meetings, leads, documents, etc.),

     * Cost (tokens + API),
     * Cost per unit (e.g. cost per meeting),

### 3.3 Group-Level Performance (Chairman Console)

Here you see:

* Top-performing agents across *all* companies,

* Underperforming agents flagged by Co-Agent.

## 5. Agent Creation & Editing – With Human-like Profiles

You want agents to feel like:

* Real personnel,
* With:

  * Birthdays,
  * Country of origin,
  * CV,
  * Personality traits.

And you want to be able to **edit agents** easily.

### 5.1 Where in the UI

Inside a Company Workspace:

* `Agents & Hierarchy` page (we already have).
* From here:

  * Click any agent → open **Agent Profile**.
  * Button: `Create New Agent`.

Also: A secondary page `Agent Directory` that lists all agents as “employees” of the company.

### 5.2 Agent Profile Structure

Every agent has:

1. **Identity**

   * Agent Name (e.g. “Aïcha – West Africa Sales Agent”)
   * Avatar (you can choose / AI-generate it)
   * Role Title (Sales Agent, Finance Agent, Legal Agent, etc.)
   * Department (Sales, Finance, Legal, Knowledge, etc.)

2. **HR-style Personal Information**

   * “Birthday” (symbolic, not real age-critical)
   * Country of origin (e.g. “Senegal”, “Ghana”, etc.)
   * Timezone they “operate” in (for scheduling logic)
   * Languages spoken (FR, EN, etc.)

3. **CV / Background**

   * Short paragraph (AI generated or edited by you):

     * “Aïcha has 10 years of experience in B2B sales across Francophone West Africa, specializing in logistics & supply chain clients.”
   * Skills list:

     * Negotiation,
     * Cold outreach,
     * CRM management,
     * French & English communication.
   * Industry focus:

     * Logistics, fintech, etc.

4. **Personality Profile**

   * Sliders or tags:

     * Tone: Formal ↔ Friendly.
     * Risk tolerance: Conservative ↔ Bold.
     * Speed: Deliberate ↔ Fast-moving.
     * Detail level: High-level ↔ Very detailed.
   * These map into the agent’s system prompts & behavior:

     * e.g. a “bold, fast, highly-detailed” Sales Agent will send more aggressive proposals with thorough information.

5. **Job Description & KPIs**

   * Mission:

     * 1–3 sentences.
   * Responsibilities:

     * List of bullet points.
   * KPIs:

     * Meetings per week,
     * Deals influenced,
     * Response rate, etc.
   * Budget & performance bonus rules:

     * Base budget,
     * Bonus per KPI unit.

6. **Tools & Permissions**

   * Email access (which account).
   * Calendar.
   * CRM.
   * Doc & knowledge base.
   * Payment or legal APIs (if relevant).
   * Web research / browsing.

7. **Autonomy Settings**

   * Draft-only vs partial vs full auto.
   * What decisions require human approval.

8. **Lifecycle**

   * Status: Active / Paused / Archived.
   * Date “hired.”
   * Date “promoted” (if hierarchy changes).
   * “Birthday celebration” (optional playful features later – e.g. AI sends you a note: “It’s Aïcha’s 1-year work anniversary, here’s a summary of what she has achieved.”)

### 5.3 Agent Editing Flow

From **Agents & Hierarchy**:

* Click on any agent node:

  * Open the full profile on the right or in a modal.
  * Editable sections:

    * Identity (name, avatar),
    * Personality,
    * KPIs & budget,
    * Tools & permissions.
* Any change:

  * Updates the agent’s system prompt & configuration.
  * Is logged in an “AgentConfigHistory”.

### 5.4 Creating a New Agent

Click: `Create Agent` → opens **Agent Creation Wizard** (“Agent Studio”).

Steps:

1. **Role Definition**

   * Ask:

     * “What do you want this agent to do?”
   * Or select from templates (Sales, Finance, Legal, etc.).
   * Co-Agent suggests a role based on company gaps.

2. **Profile & CV**

   * AI proposes:

     * Name,
     * Country of origin,
     * Background,
     * Skills.
   * You can accept or edit.

3. **Personality & Tone**

   * Slider-based:

     * Tone (Formal/Friendly),
     * Risk,
     * Speed,
     * Detail level.
   * Preview text:

     * Example of how emails/messages would sound.

4. **Manager & Hierarchy**

   * Choose:

     * Who this agent reports to.
     * Department.

5. **Permissions & Tools**

   * Checkboxes:

     * Can send emails?
     * Access CRM?
     * Schedule meetings?
     * Access finance/payments? (Careful.)
     * Web research allowed?

6. **Budget & KPI Objectives**

   * Set:

     * Initial monthly budget,
     * Target KPIs,
     * Bonus rules.

7. **Confirmation**

   * Summary page:

     * Show: profile, personality, KPIs, tools, manager.
   * Button:

     * `Create Agent`.

When you finish:

* Agent created in DB.
* Node appears in hierarchy.
* CEO Orchestrator receives a notification:

  * “New agent Aïcha hired for Francophone Sales. I’ll onboard her and assign initial tasks.”
