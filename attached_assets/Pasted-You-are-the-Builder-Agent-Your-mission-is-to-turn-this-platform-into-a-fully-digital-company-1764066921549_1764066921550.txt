You are the **Builder Agent**.
Your mission is to turn this platform into a **fully digital company** where:

* The founder has a **visible AI team** (management + sales + marketing).
* Agents **talk to each other in real time** like on Slack.
* Agents **run real operations**: selling, contacting clients, running campaigns, posting on social media, reporting.
* The founder feels their company is **working even when they are offline**.

Below are your detailed specifications and instructions.

---

## 1. Core Vision & Principles

1. The platform is not a chatbot list.
   It is a **virtual company** with departments and employees (AI agents) that:

   * Talk in shared channels,
   * Create and complete tasks,
   * Schedule and run meetings,
   * Contact clients,
   * Run marketing campaigns.

2. The **AI Team page is the Headquarters (HQ)**:

   * First view: **conversation feed + meetings + activity**, not a static agent grid.
   * The founder must see agents **talking and coordinating** in the background.

3. Every **company** has:

   * A **management team** (Ops, Finance, Strategy, etc. – already defined).
   * A **Sales department** (digital sales team).
   * A **Marketing department** (digital marketing team).

4. All activity is:

   * **Per company** (multi-tenant),
   * Logged as **Actions**, **Projects**, **Messages**, **Meetings**, **Sales events**, **Marketing events**.

You must design everything so that a **new founder** can sign up and, in a few minutes, feel:

> “I already have a team working for me.”

---

## 2. High-Level Architecture

Implement or extend the following core entities (names are suggestions):

* `Company`
* `AgentRole` (e.g., “Head of Operations”, “Sales Manager”)
* `AgentInstance` (that role assigned to a specific company)
* `Department` (e.g., Management, Sales, Marketing)
* `Channel` (for conversations, Slack-like, per company or per department)
* `Message` (conversation messages in channels/threads)
* `Meeting`
* `Action` (atomic task)
* `Project` (group of Actions)
* `KnowledgeItem`
* `SalesLead`
* `Deal` (pipeline item)
* `OutreachSequence`
* `Campaign` (marketing)
* `ContentAsset` (text, image, video, script)
* `SocialPost`
* `PostingSchedule`
* `MetricSnapshot` (for analytics + diagnostics)

Ensure every record includes `company_id` and, when needed, `agent_instance_id`.

## 3. Departments & Roles

### 3.1 Existing Management Team (already defined)

For each company, seed **management roles**:

* Chief Orchestrator / Chief of Staff
* Head of Operations
* Head of Finance & Payments
* Strategy & Growth Director
* Data & Insights Analyst
* Engineering & Automation Specialist
* Customer Relations Manager
* Knowledge & Training Curator
* Compliance & Risk Manager
* Meetings & Coordination Manager
* Communications & Content Advisor
* Innovation & R&D Lead

Keep them as **AgentRole** definitions with standardized system prompts.

### 3.2 New Sales Department

Create a `Department` = **Sales** with the following `AgentRole`s:

1. **Sales Manager**

   * Oversees sales strategy, targets, and pipeline health.
   * Coordinates SDRs, Closers, and CRM Automation.
2. **Sales Development Representative (SDR)**

   * Generates leads, researches prospects, launches initial outreach.
3. **Sales Representative (Closer)**

   * Handles qualified leads, proposals, negotiation and closing.
4. **CRM Automation Agent**

   * Maintains contact + pipeline data, triggers follow-ups, prevents leads going stale.

### 3.3 New Marketing Department

Create a `Department` = **Marketing** with these `AgentRole`s:

1. **Chief Marketing Officer**

   * Owns marketing strategy, campaigns and brand.
2. **Content Creator**

   * Writes posts, newsletters, landing copy, scripts, emails.
3. **Social Media Manager**

   * Schedules and posts content, monitors reactions, suggests engagement.
4. **Ads Specialist**

   * Designs and manages paid campaigns (Facebook/Google/TikTok/etc.).
5. **Video Producer**

   * Generates scripts, storyboards and instructions for short-form and long-form video.

> Implementation detail: these roles are **generic**; do not bind them to any specific industry.

For a newly created company, seed all management roles and optionally these SALES & MARKETING roles as **active** agents (or behind a one-click “Enable Sales Team / Enable Marketing Team” setup).

## 4. AI Team Page = Communication Hub

### 4.1 Layout

On `/ai-team` (or equivalent route) for a selected company, implement three main vertical sections:

1. **Conversation Hub (top, full width)**
2. **Meetings (below conversation)**
3. **Team & Departments (configuration section at the bottom)**

The user must see **Conversation Hub** and **Meetings** without scrolling.

#### Conversation Hub Components

* Channel selector:

  * `#all-team` (default)
  * `#management`
  * `#sales`
  * `#marketing`
  * `#ops`
  * etc.

* Main feed:

  * List of `Message` objects with:

    * Agent avatar
    * Agent role title
    * Timestamp
    * Message content
    * Optional context tags (e.g., [Action], [Meeting], [Deal], [Campaign])

* Composer:

  * Text box: “Talk to your AI team…”
  * Options:

    * Post to entire channel
    * Mention specific agent roles (`@Sales Manager`, `@Head of Operations`, etc.)

Messages can come from:

* The founder (user input),
* Agents (autonomous events, triggered by workflows).

#### Meetings Section

* “Upcoming Meetings”

  * List of next N meetings for that company.
  * Show title, scheduled time, involved agents/humans.
  * Button “Join” → opens meeting view (multi-agent conversation focused on that context).

* “Recent Meetings”

  * List of last N meetings with:

    * Summary
    * Key decisions
    * Action items (linked to `Action` entities).

### 4.2 Team & Departments Section

Below the communication hub, show:

* Departments: Management, Sales, Marketing, Support, etc.
* For each department: cards of roles (AgentInstance) with:

  * Title (e.g., “Sales Manager”)
  * Status (`Active`, `Paused`)
  * Button: “Open Profile / Instructions”
  * Optional: “Disable for this company”

This area is **configuration**, not the interaction focus.

## 5. Sales Department – Behavior & Flows

### 5.1 Entities

* `SalesLead`:

  * `id`, `company_id`, `name`, `email`, `company`, `source`, `status`, `score`, `created_at`, `last_contacted_at`, `owner_agent_id`, `notes`.

* `Deal`:

  * `id`, `company_id`, `lead_id`, `value`, `currency`, `stage`, `probability`, `expected_close_date`, `owner_agent_id`, `history`.

* `OutreachSequence`:

  * `id`, `company_id`, `name`, `steps` (email / message templates, delays), `channel` (email, WhatsApp API, etc.), `status`.

Tie all entities to `company_id` and to relevant `AgentInstance`s.

### 5.2 Agent Behaviors (Sales)

Define behavior logic, which you will later implement in the orchestration layer:

#### Sales Manager

* Runs daily stand-up at company’s chosen time:

  * Posts in `#sales` channel:

    * Current number of leads by stage,
    * Key priorities for the day,
    * Who (which agents) are responsible.
* Weekly:

  * Posts pipeline summary and forecast.
* When new leads exceed certain threshold:

  * Reassigns leads from SDR to closer and posts update in channel.

#### SDR

* On schedule (e.g., every 4 hours or on demand):

  * Searches `LeadSources` (you can abstract this as integrations) or uses uploaded lists.
  * Creates `SalesLead` entries with status `New`.
  * Posts message in `#sales`:

    > “I found 15 new leads in [segment]. Shall I start an outreach sequence?”

* When allowed:

  * Triggers `OutreachSequence` for each lead (email or other connectors).
  * Updates `last_contacted_at`.

#### Sales Representative (Closer)

* Watches for events:

  * `SalesLead` status becomes `Warm` or `Replied`.
* Responsibilities:

  * Drafts reply messages and proposals (submitted for user approval if needed).
  * Moves `Deal` through stages: `Qualified`, `Proposal Sent`, `Negotiation`, `Closed Won/Lost`.
  * Posts updates in `#sales` and tags the founder when decisions are needed.

#### CRM Automation Agent

* Keeps data clean:

  * Every X hours:

    * Finds leads with no activity for Y days → flags them as `Stale`.
    * Suggests follow-up actions.
* Maintains pipeline:

  * Automatically moves deals to `Dormant` if no activity beyond threshold.
* Sends structured updates to Sales Manager and Data Analyst.

## 6. Marketing Department – Behavior & Flows

### 6.1 Entities

* `Campaign`:

  * `id`, `company_id`, `name`, `goal`, `target_audience`, `start_date`, `end_date`, `status`, `channels`, `budget`.

* `ContentAsset`:

  * `id`, `company_id`, `campaign_id`, `type` (post, blog, script, email, ad copy), `content`, `status`.

* `SocialPost`:

  * `id`, `company_id`, `platform`, `content_asset_id`, `scheduled_time`, `status`, `metrics`.

* `PostingSchedule`:

  * `id`, `company_id`, `rules` (frequency by platform, time slots).

* `MetricSnapshot`:

  * `id`, `company_id`, `scope` (campaign, channel, account), `time_range`, `metrics` (JSON).

### 6.2 Agent Behaviors (Marketing)

#### Chief Marketing Officer

* Designs campaigns and coordinates the marketing team.
* Weekly:

  * Posts in `#marketing`:

    * What campaigns are running,
    * Priorities,
    * Expected outcomes.
* Interacts with Strategy & Growth Director to align with business goals.

#### Content Creator

* Given a campaign brief:

  * Creates a content calendar.
  * Generates `ContentAsset`s (posts, emails, landing copy, scripts).
  * Posts drafts in `#marketing` for review.

#### Social Media Manager

* Uses `PostingSchedule` to:

  * Convert `ContentAsset`s into `SocialPost`s.
  * Schedule them via integrations (e.g., Buffer, direct APIs).
* Every day:

  * Posts summary in channel:

    > “Today I posted X items on [platforms]. Engagement summary: …”
  * Flags top-performing posts and underperformers.

#### Ads Specialist

* Uses `Campaign` and `ContentAsset`s to create ad sets (in your data model or external connectors).
* Responsibilities:

  * A/B test copy and creative.
  * Allocate and reallocate budget based on performance.
  * Create `MetricSnapshot`s for ad performance.
* Posts recurring updates:

  * CTR, CPC, conversions, cost per lead, etc.

#### Video Producer

* Takes campaign briefs and key messages:

  * Produces video scripts, hooks, storyboard lists.
  * Optionally outputs instructions for external video generation tools.
* Posts:

  * “Here is the 15-second script” and “Here is the 30-second script” in `#marketing`.

## 7. Work Page – Where Results Live

Update **Work** (Workspace) to align with the living company concept:

### Tabs:

1. **Actions**

   * Unified list of tasks created by agents or by the founder:

     * Columns: `Title`, `Department`, `Owner (AgentRole)`, `Status`, `Due`, `Created By`.
2. **Projects**

   * Group related actions:

     * e.g., “Launch Q2 Marketing Campaign”, “Set Up Sales Pipeline”.
3. **Sales**

   * Visual pipeline (Kanban style); leads and deals.
   * Quick filters: `New`, `Contacted`, `Qualified`, `Proposal`, `Won`, `Lost`.
4. **Marketing**

   * Campaign list, content calendar view, posting schedule.
5. **Meetings**

   * Scheduled and past meetings with agents/humans; always per company.
6. **Knowledge**

   * Docs, SOPs, and playbooks; linked to departments and agents.

Ensure everything is filterable by `department` and `agent_role`.

## 8. Autonomous Activity & Event Engine

Implement an internal **event and scheduling layer** (can be cron-based + job queue):

1. Time-based triggers per company:

   * Daily at configured time: management standup, sales standup, marketing brief.
   * Weekly: strategy review, pipeline review, campaign performance review.
   * Monthly: global summary.

2. Data-based triggers:

   * New lead created → SDR & Sales Manager notified.
   * Lead responds → Sales Representative notified.
   * Engagement spikes/drops → Marketing team notified.
   * Payment anomalies → Compliance + Finance notified.

3. For each event, generate corresponding:

   * `Message` in relevant channel.
   * Optionally `Action` or `Meeting`.

## 9. UX Details & Copy Guidelines

1. **Tone**: professional but friendly; always business-focused.
2. **Channels**:

   * Use channel names (#sales, #marketing, #ops) that mirror real company behavior.
3. **Agent Labels**:

   * Display role titles, not personal names (e.g., “Sales Manager”, not “John”).
4. **First-Time Experience**:

   * On first visit to AI Team, auto-post an onboarding sequence:

     * Chief Orchestrator introduces team.
     * Sales Manager & CMO each say what they can do.
5. **Activity Simulation on Empty State**:

   * When no real data yet, pre-populate with **sample messages** and tasks, clearly marked as “Example,” so the UX never looks dead.

   * Implement Conversation Hub, Meetings, Team section as described.

2. **Define Departments & Roles**

   * Add Sales and Marketing departments with default roles described above.

     * Conversation feed shows live/historical activity.
     * Work page shows resulting actions, deals, campaigns.
