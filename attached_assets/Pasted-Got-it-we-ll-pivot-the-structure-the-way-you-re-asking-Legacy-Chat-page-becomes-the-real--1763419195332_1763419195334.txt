## 3. Start, Run, and Stop Meetings

### 3.1 `New Meeting` (Instant)

From the Meetings page, top left:

**Button:** `New Meeting`

Flow:

1. Dialog:

   * Title (optional, default: “Untitled Meeting with Agents”)
   * Select participants:

     * Chairman (default, cannot unselect)
     * Chairman’s Assistant (optional)
     * Any agents
   * Mode:

     * `Start Now` (default)

2. On confirm:

   * Create a new Meeting:

     * `status = Live`
     * `startTime = now`
   * It appears in the left list.
   * It opens in the center chat.

You now talk directly to those agents in that room.

### 3.2 `New Meeting` (Scheduled)

Same dialog, but:

* Mode: `Schedule`
* Ask:

  * Date
  * Time
  * Duration

On confirm:

* `status = Scheduled`
* Link to calendar (if integrated)
* Meeting card appears in:

  * “Scheduled” filter
* When time comes and you click it:

  * `status` becomes `Live`
  * Meeting room opens.

### 3.3 Ending a Meeting

In the meeting header:

* `End Meeting` button:

  * Sets `status = Completed`
  * Registers end time
  * Triggers summary generation (later)
* Meeting becomes **read-only** transcript.
