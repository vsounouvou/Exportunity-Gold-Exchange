# ✅ **1. THE COMPANY MODULE — THE CORE IDEA**

Every user is a **Chairman** who can create, edit, and manage **multiple companies** inside one account.

Each company has:

### ✔️ A Profile

(Name, mission, sector, country, bylaws, legal model…)

### ✔️ A Vision

(Long-term plan, short-term goals, KPIs, milestones…)

### ✔️ A Structure

(Departments, agents, org chart, subsidiaries…)

### ✔️ A Budget

(Fixed or dynamic — used by the AI to act)

### ✔️ A Dashboard

(Financials, activity, actions executed, meetings, performance, calendar…)

### ✔️ A Knowledge Base

(Documents, SOPs, strategy docs, reports…)

### ✔️ A Legal & Compliance layer

(Tax rules based on country, incorporation type, shareholders…)

### ✔️ AI Behavior Settings

(How autonomous agents behave inside this company)

# ✅ **2. COMPANY PAGE – WHAT INFORMATION MUST EXIST**

When the user clicks on any company, they go to this page:

---

## **A. COMPANY HEADER**

* **Company Name**
* **Logo / Icon**
* **Country of Registration**
* **Legal Type**

  * LLC / FZ-LLC / SA / SARL / FZE / etc.
* **Created on (Date)**
* **Status:** Active / Testing / Paused / Archived

---

## **B. COMPANY OVERVIEW BLOCK**

This includes:

### **1. Description / What the company does**

Clear text field describing the company.

### **2. Sector & Industry Category**

Examples:

* Trade & Export
* Gold and Precious Metals
* Education
* Logistics
* Construction
* Architecture
* Technology / AI
* Retail / E-commerce
* Media
* Agriculture
* Real Estate

User can choose:

* **Primary sector**
* **Secondary sector**
* **Industry tags**

Agents will use this to adjust behavior.

---

## **C. VISION & STRATEGY BLOCK**

### ✔️ Company Vision (Long-term)

e.g.
*"Become the leading gold export partner between West Africa and the UAE."*

### ✔️ Current Goals (Short-term)

e.g.

* “Setup a procurement office in Abidjan”
* “Sign 5 suppliers”
* “Automate email outreach”

### ✔️ KPIs

* Revenue
* Leads
* Deals closed
* Actions executed
* Meetings held
* Autonomous tasks completed

Users can manually edit these — or the Chairman Assistant can auto-generate them.

---

## **D. BUDGET, FINANCE & OPERATIONS BLOCK**

### ✔️ AI Operational Budget

Amount the Chairman assigns.

* Monthly budget
* Daily cash burn target
* Token usage limit
* API cost controls
* Spending categories (ads, email credits, research…)

### ✔️ Revenue Tracking

* Revenue generated
* Profit
* Expenses
* AI-suggested optimizations

### ✔️ Shareholding & Investors

Table:

| Shareholder | % | Role | Wallet/account | Distribution mode |

Investors can be added directly.

### ✔️ Auto-distribution Settings

* Revenue → Split by shareholding
* Expenses → Covered by Chairman or company wallet
* Taxes → Based on company country

---

## **E. SUBSIDIARIES BLOCK**

User can add:

* Subsidiary name
* Country
* Purpose
* Agents assigned
* Separate or shared budget
* Separate or shared knowledge base

Agents can suggest:
“Chairman, I recommend we create a Delaware entity for this product.”

---

## **F. AGENTS & ORGANIZATION BLOCK**

This is the **company-specific hierarchy**.

Includes:

* Chairman Assistant (always present)
* Core departments:

  * Marketing
  * Sales
  * Legal & Compliance
  * Finance
  * Support
  * HR
  * Operations
* Custom departments
* Agents inside each department
* Subsidiary agents
* External integrations (APIs, email, CRM, LinkedIn)

Inside this block:

### “Add Agent” Button

Chairman or Chairman Assistant can create:

* Executive AI
* Marketing AI
* Sales AI
* Research AI
* Admin AI
* Compliance AI

Each with:

* Name
* Country of origin (for personality/cultural alignment)
* Personality
* Avatar
* Skillset
* Permissions (Action assignation)

---

## **G. KNOWLEDGE BASE BLOCK**

Shows:

* Company documents
* SOPs
* AI-generated reports
* PDFs
* Strategy files
* Market research

With:

* Add document
* Add web link
* Add text note
* Organize in collections

---

## **H. MEETINGS / COMMUNICATION BLOCK**

Displays:

* Meetings held today
* Meetings scheduled
* Agent-to-agent meetings
* Meetings between Chairman Assistant & Chairman
* Action logs
* Daily diary view (day collapsible system)

---

## **I. COMPANY SETTINGS BLOCK**

* Name change
* Country change
* Logo
* Theme color
* AI behavior configuration

  * Autonomy level
  * Risk appetite
  * Creativity
  * Strictness
* Legal info
* Billing info
* API integrations (Google, LinkedIn, Email)

# ✅ **5. HOW THE CHAIRMAN ASSISTANT USES COMPANY DATA**

The Chairman Assistant will:

* Read the company's sector → adapt strategy
* Read goals → create meetings & tasks
* Read budget → plan agent actions within cost limits
* Read vision → build multi-year plans
* Read documents → update knowledge
* Read shareholding → calculate distributions
* Read country → enforce legal rules
* Read subsidiaries → structure tasks

When a company is created, it triggers:

* Setup of departments
* Setup of default agents
* Creation of knowledge folders
* Creation of first meeting with Chairman
* Daily AI diary startup
* Calendar entries (“morning briefing”, “evening summary”)
