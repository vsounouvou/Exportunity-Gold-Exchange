# UX Rebuild Plan

## Product Principle

Exportunity is a commerce platform first. The user arrives to find and buy products, discover nearby shops, source wholesale goods, and review verified SMEs. AI supports the task; it does not replace the marketplace.

## Desktop Shell

- Left pane: Tassi or the active shop/business agent, one composer, quick suggestions, voice/photo controls.
- Center pane: products, merchant stories, shop entry, orders, wholesale quote flow, or My Business conversation.
- Right pane: live contextual map with user location, markers, and route line.
- `/map` remains map-dominant for users who explicitly request map view.

## Mobile Shell

- Product/shop discovery remains primary.
- Map becomes supporting and expandable.
- Shop entry shows products immediately.
- Tassi and shop agents remain accessible through one conversation composer.

## Shop Flow

1. User taps a product, shop card, or marker.
2. Quick peek opens with shop identity, product examples, distance, and ETA.
3. User enters the shop.
4. Shop Front Desk agent takes over from Tassi.
5. Products and order controls take most of the view.
6. Delivery and Wallet agents join only when useful.

## Wholesale Flow

1. User opens Wholesale.
2. Center shows supplier offers and quote-oriented cards.
3. Map shows supplier/logistics context.
4. Quote requests collect quantity, location, urgency, and supplier candidates.
5. Outreach remains approval-gated.

## Bourse de PME Flow

1. User opens Ready export / Bourse de PME.
2. Center shows merchant profiles, trust signals, products, proof, and story.
3. Investment readiness remains internal-review until compliance approval.
