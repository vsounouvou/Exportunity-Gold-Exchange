## 2. Dashboard Page – What It Does

**Goal:**
Show, for any period (day / week / month):

* What conversations and meetings happened,
* What **actions** were taken,
* What **decisions** were made,
* What **money** was generated or moved,
* All organized by **date** and **category**.

So it’s both:

* A **calendar of your AI company**, and
* A **business dashboard of outcomes**.

## 3. Dashboard Layout

Think in three vertical zones:

### 3.1 Top: KPIs Summary (Money & Activity)

At the top of the Dashboard:

    * Their actions, decisions, money impact.

Optional:

### 3.3 Bottom: Daily Log (Organized by Category)

When a date is selected:

* Show a **list grouped by category**, for that day:

Example categories:

* **Strategy**
* **Sales / Business Development**
* **Marketing**
* **Finance**
* **Operations**
* **Compliance / Legal**
* **Product / Tech**
* **Agent-only syncs**

For each category, show:

Buttons:

## 4. How Categories & Data Are Built

Behind the scenes, after each conversation/meeting:

1. The system summarizes:

   * Main **topic(s)** → assign one or more **categories**.
   * **Decisions** made.
   * **Actions** executed or assigned.
   * **Money impact** (if any: revenue, cost, budgets, etc.).

2. These are saved as structured fields on the **Conversation / Meeting**:

```ts
type ConversationSummary = {
  conversationId: string;
  date: string;
  categories: string[]; // ['Sales', 'Finance']
  decisions: string[];
  actions: string[];
  money: {
    revenueGenerated?: number;
    pipelineCreated?: number;
    budgetChanged?: number;
  };
};
```

3. The **Dashboard** reads from these summaries:

   * Aggregates money & counts (for KPIs),
   * Groups by category,
   * Displays per-day logs under the calendar.
